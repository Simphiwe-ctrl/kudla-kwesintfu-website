About Swati Cuisine

Swati traditional food reflects the culture and history of the Kingdom of Eswatini. Most meals are prepared using locally grown crops such as maize, pumpkins, beans and leafy vegetables. Families often gather to share meals during celebrations, ceremonies and community events. Traditional cooking methods preserve the natural flavours and nutritional value of the food.

Popular Swati Traditional Foods

Sidvudvu (Pumpkin)

A delicious sweet pumpkin dish often served with maize meal. It is one of the most popular traditional meals in Eswatini.

Pap (Maize Meal)

Pap is the staple food in Eswatini. It is enjoyed with meat, vegetables or sour milk.

Beans

Beans are rich in protein and are commonly cooked with maize or served alongside pap.

Wild Spinach (Imifino)

Fresh leafy vegetables cooked with onions and tomatoes, providing important vitamins and minerals.

Grilled Meat

Beef, goat or chicken are commonly prepared during celebrations and family gatherings.

Sour Milk (Emasi)

A traditional fermented milk enjoyed with pap. It has a creamy texture and unique flavour.
